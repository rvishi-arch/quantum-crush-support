Quantum Crush

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

Quantum Crush is a single-player tile-matching puzzle game developed by Vexalo Studios. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

No Account Required

Quantum Crush does not require you to create an account or sign in. Your level progress, currency (gems), and settings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (gem bundles and a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase). These purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details — Apple's own privacy policy governs that information.

Advertising

Quantum Crush does not currently display any advertising. If ads are introduced in a future update, this policy will be updated beforehand to describe the ad network used and what data it collects.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any analytics, advertising-tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs in this app, and we do not collect device identifiers, location data, or any personally identifiable information.

Children's Privacy

Quantum Crush is rated for ages 9+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add advertising or analytics in a future update — we will update this page and revise the "last updated" date above.
