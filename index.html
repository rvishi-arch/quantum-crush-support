Common Questions

I lost progress / my level didn't save

Progress is stored on your device. Make sure the app has permission to run in the background and isn't being force-closed by battery optimization settings, then relaunch. If the issue continues, email us with your device model and iOS version.

I made a purchase but didn't receive my gems/boosters

Open the Shop screen and tap "Restore Purchases." If that doesn't resolve it, email us with your purchase receipt (from the App Store) and we'll sort it out.

I found a bug

Let us know the level number, what happened, and (if possible) a screenshot — it helps us fix it faster.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Quantum Crush doesn't require sign-in — your progress is saved locally on your device.
